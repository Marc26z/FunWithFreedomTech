# Glow App Is For Everybody
![Glow Wallet Is For Everybody](https://github.com/Marc26z/FunWithFreedomTech/blob/main/public/blog/images/glowIsForEverybody/GlowIsForEverybody.jpg?raw=true)

Testing Bitcoin Lightning wallets, for me, is better than watching baseball🥱. The Lightning Network is like email for payments. Brick-and-mortar businesses are beginning to accept payments over this monetary email network.

I use this monetary network by setting aside a certain number of sats every month that I call my blow money. The term is borrowed from the nocoiner known for saying “Cash is King,” Dave Ramsey.

In the old days, people thought you could only use Bitcoin to buy drugs, but the only drug I ever bought with the cryptocurrency was coffee. I use it to buy computers, gift cards, burgers, and non-alcoholic beer*. You can buy real beer with it too, but I don’t drink that stuff. This blow money is the part of my budget I allow myself to spend on whatever I want with no judgment. For those sats, I use [Glow Wallet](https://glow.app/) made by the Breez team. They also make the [Misty Wallet, which I wrote about here](https://primal.net/marc/misty-wallet-review).

## Glow Wallet Has Passkey Support

![PassKey](https://github.com/Marc26z/FunWithFreedomTech/blob/main/public/blog/images/glowIsForEverybody/PassKey.jpg?raw=true)
**The [passkey](https://www.google.com/account/about/passkeys/) is the easiest self-custodial onboarding experience I've ever seen**. It makes backups as easy as a click of a button. Again, this is my blow money, not my generational wealth. The passkey makes it easy to restore or use on multiple devices because the passkey is stored in your Google account. Think this sounds [sketch](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=sketch)? No problem. You can use other passkeys too, but this is an easy-button setup for a hot wallet. **The easy button is great for lunch money**. This is blow money I plan on spending anyway, not sats I have earmarked for my grandchildren. It's sats I spend on coffee and non-alcoholic beer. Could I stack more sats if I stopped drinking coffee and near beer? Yes, but it's nice to splurge a couple times every 4,320 blocks (30 days). Besides, if a corporation can buy dollars with bitcoin, plebs can buy coffee.

### BIP 39 Support
![BIP-39 Support](https://github.com/Marc26z/FunWithFreedomTech/blob/main/public/blog/images/glowIsForEverybody/BIP39Too.jpg?raw=true)

Glow App also has a [BIP39](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki) backup! Google Passkey is not required. You can write down 12 words instead. Each word actually represents a four-digit number like you might use to withdraw paper money with your debit card. Therefore, a 12-word backup is like 12 PINs required to spend your money.

Even if the wallet fails, you can import these 12 words into another wallet and restore your balance, at least the bitcoin. *I'm not sure how stablecoin backups work*. Make sure you double-check though. The words must be in order. If you make a mistake and never recover your password, consider those sats a donation to the network. You can do that if you want, but **it is impossible to spend sats time-locked with a lost key. Start with lunch money, not life-changing money**.

This is a great wallet to begin your self-custody journey*. Brown-bag your lunch one day. Sell the fiat you save for bitcoin. Use that lunch money to learn self-custody. If you lose 12,000 sats, hopefully, it's not the end of the world.

#### Glow Has A Progressive Web App
![Glow Is An App](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Marc26z/FunWithFreedomTech/96176642e3225138c6a29382d4dd79361a8e5c6f/public/blog/images/glowIsForEverybody/GlowIsAnApp.jpg)

You can use Glow as both a PWA or an app you download from the App Store or Google Play. This means you can access the wallet even if it is taken off the application stores. PWA, for non-shadowy super coders, stands for **Progressive Web App. It sounds like tax policy, but it's a fancy name that means an app on a web browser**.

This progressive web app can be used to restore your wallet on a phone, computer, or laptop. This is one of the sexiest things about the wallet from my perspective. I've been looking for a PWA that has a Lightning address for some time. The fact that most wallets are locked behind the walled garden of the major stores concerns me. Designing it to be used as a PWA makes it more decentralized. App-only apps are easy to shut down if asked by an ABC department.

*How do I get bitcoin?* There is only one way.

1. Sell something for bitcoin. This year, I met a dude who sold baklava and cleaned toilets to get bitcoin. He brings free baklava to the conference now. Nowadays, some companies allow you to convert your paycheck (or a certain percentage of it) into bitcoin. I'm not an economist, but this is selling your labor for bitcoin. **Most bitcoiners sell digital dollars for bitcoin**. They get paid in dollars. They sell those dollars for bitcoin. **The Glow App makes it easy to sell dollars for bitcoin on Cash App or Moonpay**. *Or... you can just get paid in bitcoin on Cash App*. Is this KYC-free? No, but the lunch lady won't know where you got your sats from when you use the Lightning Network ... unless you work for the NSA😉.
