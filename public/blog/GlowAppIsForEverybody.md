### BIP 39 Support
![BIP-39 Suport](https://github.com/Marc26z/FunWithFreedomTech/blob/main/public/blog/images/glowIsForEverybody/BIP39Too.jpg?raw=true)

Glow App also has a [BIP39](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki) backup!  Google passkey not required. You can  write down 12 words instead. Each word actually represents a 4 digit number like you might use to withdraw paper money with your Debit card. Therefore a 12 word backup is like 12 PIN's required to spend your money. 

Even if the wallet fails, you can import these 12 words onto another wallet and restore your balance, at least the bitcoin. *I'm not sure how stablecoin backups work*. Make sure you double check though. The words must be in order. If you make a mistake and never recover your password, consider those sats as a dontation to the network. . You can do that if you want, but **it is impossible spend sats time-locked with a lost key. Start with lunch money, not life-changing money**.
This is a great wallet to begin your self custody journey*. Brown bag your lunch one day. Sell the fiat you save for bitcoin. Use that lunch money to learn self custody. If you lose ten 12,000 sats, hopefully, it's not the end of the world.  

#### Glow Has A Progessive Web App

You can use Glow as both a  PWA or an app you download on the App store or Google Play. This means you can access the wallet even if it is taken off of the application stores. PWA, for non-shadowy super coders stands for **Progressive Web App. It sounds like tax policy, but it's a fancy hame that means app on a web browser**. 

This progressive web app can be used to restore your wallet on a phone, computer, or laptop. This is one of the sexiest things about the wallet from my perspective. I've been looking for a PWA that has a lightning address for some time. The fact that most wallets are locked behind the walled garden of the major stores concerns me. Designing it to be used as a PWA makes it more decentralized. App only apps are easy to shut down if asked by an ABC department.
